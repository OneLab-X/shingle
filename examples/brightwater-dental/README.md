# Brightwater Family Dental (example)

> Fictional practice. Generated by Shingle from [`business.json`](business.json) to show the
> **dental-clinic** category. The phone number is in the US 555-01xx fiction range; the domain
> uses the reserved `.example` TLD.

| | |
|---|---|
| Category | `dental-clinic` → schema.org `Dentist` |
| Pages | Multi-page: Home, Services, New patients, Contact (+ breadcrumbs JSON-LD) |
| Direction | **Clinical Calm**: Figtree + Atkinson Hyperlegible, 12px radius, soft panels |
| Palette | **Mint Clinic** (light + dark) |
| Primary CTA | Call to book (no online booking link was given) |

## Design rationale
People visiting a dentist's site are often anxious, in a hurry, or on a phone with toothache.
So the design optimises for calm and legibility: Atkinson Hyperlegible body text at 18px,
generous spacing, two columns at most, and plain soft panels instead of decoration. The
**signature element** is the live "Open now · until 6 pm" status in the top bar of every page
(computed in the practice's time zone) plus a sticky Call / Directions bar on phones, so the
two things patients need most are never more than a thumb away. Mint and teal feel fresh rather
than clinical; the marigold accent marks the one urgent panel (emergencies).

## Facts vs placeholders
Services, team names and roles, insurance and payment wording, access info and FAQ all come
from `business.json`. Not invented: bios, qualifications beyond what was given, prices,
