# Examples

Each folder is a complete site generated by Shingle from its `business.json`, built and audited
in CI. All businesses are fictional: phone numbers come from ranges reserved for fiction
(Ofcom, ACMA, North American 555-01xx) and domains use the reserved `.example` TLD.
Together they cover all eight design directions, and `barro-e-luz` shows a two-language (pt + en) site.

| Example | Category | Direction | Palette | Pages |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Marigold Kitchen](marigold-kitchen/) | restaurant | Broadsheet | Saffron & Ink | 1 |
| [Brightwater Family Dental](brightwater-dental/) | dental-clinic | Clinical Calm | Mint Clinic | 4 |
| [Ridgeback Plumbing & Gas](ridgeback-plumbing/) | contractor-home-services | Swiss Grid | Safety Orange | 1 |
| [Little Wren Coffee](little-wren-coffee/) | cafe | Handmade | Espresso & Cream | 1 |
| [Forge Strength Club](forge-strength/) | gym-fitness | Poster | Volt Graphite | 1 |
| [Northlight Studio](northlight-studio/) | photographer | Gallery | Darkroom | 1 |
| [Linden Hair & Skin](linden-hair/) | salon-spa | Quiet Luxury | Rose Clay | 1 |
| [Compass & Kite Journeys](compass-kite/) | travel-agency | Wayfinder | Lagoon | 1 |
| [Barro & Luz](barro-e-luz/) | ecommerce-boutique | Handmade | Terracotta Sun | 1 per language (pt, en) |
| [Harbour Loaf Bakery](harbour-loaf-bakery/) | cafe | Handmade | Espresso & Cream | 1 (real-photo path: imported test images) |

Build one: `cd <example> && npm install && npm run build`, then
`python3 ../../skills/shingle/scripts/audit_site.py dist`.
