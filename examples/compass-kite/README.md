## Design rationale
A slow-travel studio is about the journey, so the page is one. The **signature element** is a
dotted route line running down the left edge, linking numbered map pins at each section
(Start → Routes we love → How it works → Destination) and ending at the contact details; in
browsers that support scroll-driven animation it extends as you scroll. Trips are ticket cards
with a dashed stub for the number of days and the season. Deep lagoon teal with a coral accent
feels coastal without falling back on sunset gradients or stock beaches.

## Facts vs placeholders
Trips, routes, seasons, the planning process and the pricing statement come from
`business.json`. Not invented: prices, hotel or airline partners, "best price" promises,
reviews. Still to fill:
