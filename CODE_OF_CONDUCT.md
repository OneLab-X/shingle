# Code of Conduct

Shingle adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. In short:

- Be welcoming, kind and patient. Assume good intent.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- No harassment, discrimination, sexualised language or imagery, personal attacks, or
  publishing others' private information.
- Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and comments that break these rules,
  and may ban contributors for repeated or serious violations.

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing
the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers at **[onelabx@yahoo.com](mailto:onelabx@yahoo.com)** or through GitHub's "Report content" option. Reports are handled confidentially,
and the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 apply.
