# Category: dental-clinic

General and family dentistry, orthodontics, cosmetic dental practices, dental hygiene clinics.

- **Audience:** new patients comparing practices (often anxious), existing patients needing
  hours or the phone number, parents booking for children, people with a dental emergency.
- **Tone options:** Calm and reassuring (default) · Friendly and upbeat · Precise and clinical.
- **Schema type:** `Dentist`. Add `medicalSpecialty` only if the owner names one.
- **Default pages:** multi-page: Home, Services, New patients, Contact. Single page only for a
  very small practice with 3 or fewer services.
- **Primary CTA options:** Book an appointment (default; booking link or phone) · Call the
  practice · Emergency appointment (when the practice offers them).

## Required sections
1. Hero: practice name, what + where, open status, Book/Call CTA. Emergency line if offered.
2. Services: only services the owner lists, each with a plain-language summary.
3. New patients: what to bring, forms, insurance/payment info from facts.
4. Hours, address, map, parking/accessibility info if provided.
5. Contact: phone first, email, booking link.

## Optional sections (rank by fit)
Team (names and roles from facts; bios only from the owner) · FAQ (from facts) · Emergency
care · Payment plans (only real ones) · Accessibility (step-free access, languages spoken) ·
Patient reviews (only owner-supplied with consent; never scraped or invented).

## Category questions (ask at most 4, skip any answered)
- "How do patients book?" Phone (Recommended if no link) / Online booking link / Both.
- "Do you see emergency patients?" Yes, same day when possible / Yes, dedicated line / No.
- "What should the services page cover?" Pre-filled from facts; ask only if none given.
- "Insurance and payment info?" List plans accepted (owner's list) / "Call us to check"
  (Recommended if unsure) / Leave out.

## Imagery
Calm, bright, real. Slots: reception or treatment room (3:2), team portraits (4:5) only when
the owner sends them. Prefer typography and soft surfaces over stock smiles. No tooth clip
art, no before/after photos without the owner's documented patient consent.

## Never invent
Dentists' names, qualifications, or memberships · years of experience · "painless" or outcome
guarantees · prices or fees · insurance plans accepted · patient reviews or ratings ·
technology claims (lasers, 3D scanners) · emergency availability · accreditation logos.

## Compliance notes
Health advertising rules vary by country (claims, testimonials, before/after images). Keep
copy factual, avoid superlatives and guarantees, and flag any claim you are unsure about as a
TODO for the owner to confirm.

## Design fit
Directions: `clinical-calm` (default), `swiss-grid` (modern practice), `quiet-luxury`
(cosmetic). Palettes: `mint-clinic`, `harbor-blue`, `sage-linen`.

## Copy notes
- Reassure with specifics, not adjectives: "We explain every step before we start" only if
  the owner says it; otherwise describe the services plainly.
- Put the phone number and today's hours in the header on every page.
- New patients page answers: what to bring, how long the first visit takes (if known), how to
  pay.
