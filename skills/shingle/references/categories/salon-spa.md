# Category: salon-spa

Hair salons, barbers, nail studios, beauty and skin clinics (non-medical), day spas, massage.

- **Audience:** locals choosing where to book, regulars rebooking, people checking prices and
  whether a specific treatment is offered.
- **Tone options:** Refined and quiet (default) · Warm and friendly · Bold and fashion-forward.
- **Schema type:** most specific of `HairSalon`, `BarberShop` (use `HairSalon`), `NailSalon`,
  `BeautySalon`, `DaySpa`, `HealthAndBeautyBusiness` via `decisions.schema_type`.
- **Default pages:** single page; multi-page when the treatment menu is long (20+ items).
- **Primary CTA options:** Book online (default when a booking link exists) · Call to book ·
  Message on WhatsApp/Instagram.

## Required sections
1. Hero: name, what + where, booking CTA.
2. Treatments/services with prices or "from" prices from facts; TODO when missing.
3. Booking info: how to book, cancellation policy only if stated.
4. Hours + location + map.

## Optional sections (rank by fit)
The team/stylists (names from facts) · Gallery (owner's own work only) · Products used (only
stated brands) · Gift vouchers (real link) · FAQ · Accessibility.

## Category questions (ask at most 4, skip any answered)
- "How do clients book?" Online booking link (ask) / Phone (Recommended if no link) / Instagram DMs.
- "Show prices?" Yes, from prices (Recommended if given) / Price on consultation / Leave TODOs.
- "Cancellation or deposit policy to show?" Enter it / None / Leave out (Recommended if unsure).
- "Do you want stylists listed?" Yes, names and specialities / No.

## Imagery
Their own work, the space, the light. Portrait crops (4:5). Never stock models or
before/after photos the owner did not take with client permission.

## Never invent
Prices · product brands · stylist names or experience · qualifications or certifications ·
"award-winning" · treatment outcomes (e.g. "reduces wrinkles") · reviews · deposits/policies.

## Design fit
Directions: `quiet-luxury` (default), `gallery`, `handmade`. Palettes: `rose-clay`,
`sage-linen`, `boutique-plum`, `lagoon`.

## Copy notes
Fewer words, specific ones. Treatment names exactly as the salon uses them; durations if given.
