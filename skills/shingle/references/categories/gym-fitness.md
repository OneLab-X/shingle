# Category: gym-fitness

Gyms, strength clubs, boxing and martial arts, yoga and pilates studios, CrossFit boxes,
personal trainers, climbing walls.

- **Audience:** people deciding whether to try a first class (nervous or motivated), members
  checking the timetable, people comparing membership options.
- **Tone options:** Bold and energetic (default) · Friendly and welcoming · Calm and focused (yoga).
- **Schema type:** `ExerciseGym`; yoga/pilates `SportsActivityLocation`; via `decisions.schema_type`.
- **Default pages:** single page. Multi-page with a long timetable or many programmes.
- **Primary CTA options:** Book a first class / trial (only if offered) · Join · Call or message us.

## Required sections
1. Hero: name, what you train + where, the first-step CTA.
2. Classes/programmes from `facts.classes`, timetable if provided.
3. Memberships/pricing from facts or TODO; trial offer only if stated.
4. Hours + location + map, parking/transport if provided.
5. Contact.

## Optional sections (rank by fit)
Coaches (names and certifications from facts) · What to expect on your first visit · FAQ ·
Facilities (only stated) · Kids/teens classes · Community/events.

## Category questions (ask at most 4, skip any answered)
- "What is the first step for a newcomer?" Free trial (only if true) / Book a paid intro /
  Just turn up / Call us (Recommended if unsure).
- "Show membership prices?" Yes (owner's prices) / "Ask us" (Recommended if not given).
- "Show the class timetable?" Yes from facts / Link to the booking app / No.
- "Words that describe the place?" Free text, used for the marquee and headings verbatim.

## Imagery
Real members training (with consent), the floor, the kit. Action crops. Without photos, big
type and colour bands do the work. Never stock fitness models or before/after body photos.

## Never invent
Prices · trial offers · coach names, qualifications, competition results · member numbers ·
weight-loss or health outcomes · equipment lists · opening year · reviews · "24/7" access.

## Design fit
Directions: `poster` (default), `swiss-grid`, `clinical-calm` (yoga/pilates). Palettes:
`volt-graphite`, `ember-steel`, `safety-orange`.

## Copy notes
Short lines, verbs, no shaming. Talk to beginners as much as to regulars.
