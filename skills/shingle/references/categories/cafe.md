# Category: cafe

Coffee shops, bakeries, tea rooms, brunch spots, juice bars, ice cream shops.

- **Audience:** regulars checking today's hours, neighbours looking for a place to sit or work,
  visitors searching "coffee near me" on a phone.
- **Tone options:** Warm and neighbourly (default) · Playful · Quiet and crafted.
- **Schema type:** `CafeOrCoffeeShop` (bakeries: `Bakery`; ice cream: `IceCreamShop`) via
  `decisions.schema_type`. `servesCuisine` and `hasMenu` from facts.
- **Default pages:** single page.
- **Primary CTA options:** Get directions (default: walk-in business) · Order ahead (only with a
  real link) · Call us · See the menu.

## Required sections
1. Hero: name, what it is + neighbourhood, open now / today's hours.
2. Menu highlights from `facts.menu` (drinks, food, bakes) with real prices or TODOs.
3. Hours + location with map and a one-line "how to find us" if provided.
4. Contact: phone, email, socials.

## Optional sections (rank by fit)
About/story · What makes it different (only stated facts: roaster, bakes on site) ·
Wi-Fi/laptop policy · Dogs welcome / accessibility · Catering and wholesale (only if offered) ·
Gallery (owner photos) · Events.

## Category questions (ask at most 4, skip any answered)
- "What should people see first?" Today's hours and directions (Recommended) / The menu / The space.
- "Do you take orders ahead?" No, walk in (Recommended if no link) / Yes, online (ask link) / By phone.
- "Anything regulars love?" Free text: signature drink, bakes, roaster. Use only what they say.
- "Laptops, dogs, accessibility?" Multi-select of stated policies; skip if not relevant.

## Imagery
The counter, the bakes, the cups, the window seat. Square crops suit menus. Without photos,
hand-drawn SVG accents and an hours stamp carry the warmth. Never stock latte art.

## Never invent
Prices · roaster or supplier names · "organic", "fair trade", "locally sourced" · awards ·
"best coffee in town" · reviews · vegan or allergen guarantees · Wi-Fi or dog policies.

## Design fit
Directions: `handmade` (default), `broadsheet`, `poster` (bold, young brands). Palettes:
`espresso-cream`, `olive-grove`, `meadow-hope`, `terracotta-sun`.

## Copy notes
Short and friendly. The hours and the address matter more than the story. Name drinks and
bakes plainly ("cardamom bun") and let the specifics do the selling.
