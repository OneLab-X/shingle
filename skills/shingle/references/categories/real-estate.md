# Category: real-estate

Estate agents, realtors, letting and property management agencies, independent brokers.

- **Audience:** sellers and landlords choosing an agent, buyers and renters browsing the area,
  existing clients needing contact details.
- **Tone options:** Confident and local (default) · Refined and quiet · Friendly and plain.
- **Schema type:** `RealEstateAgent` with `areaServed`.
- **Default pages:** multi-page: Home, Sell/Let, Buy/Rent, About, Contact. Listings are linked
  to the agent's portal or feed; Shingle does not build a property database.
- **Primary CTA options:** Book a valuation (default for sellers) · View listings (link) · Call the office.

## Required sections
1. Hero: agency name, areas covered, the main CTA.
2. Services: selling, letting, management, as the owner offers them.
3. Areas covered (from `service_area`).
4. Listings: link out to the portal/feed URL the owner gives, or a TODO.
5. Contact, office hours, address, map.

## Optional sections (rank by fit)
Team (names, roles, licence numbers from facts) · Fees (only stated) · Area guides written from
facts · Valuation request form (form service) · Testimonials (owner-supplied, permission).

## Category questions (ask at most 4, skip any answered)
- "Who is the site mainly for?" Sellers and landlords (Recommended) / Buyers and renters / Both.
- "Where do listings live?" Portal or IDX link (ask) / No listings on site.
- "Licence or registration numbers to show?" Enter / TODO (Recommended).
- "Valuation requests by form?" Yes via form service / Phone and email only.

## Imagery
Owner's property photos (with rights), the office, the team. Wide crops. Never photos of
properties the agency is not marketing, never stock skylines.

## Never invent
Sales figures, "sold in X days", market statistics · fees · licence numbers · awards or
rankings · listings · testimonials · team members.

## Design fit
Directions: `swiss-grid` (default), `quiet-luxury` (high-end), `wayfinder`. Palettes:
`estate-stone`, `counsel-navy`, `blueprint`, `gallery-white`.

## Copy notes
Local knowledge in specifics the owner gives (streets, schools, transport) and nothing else.
