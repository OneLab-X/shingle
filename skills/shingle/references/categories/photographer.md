# Category: photographer

Wedding, portrait, family, commercial, product and event photographers; videographers.

- **Audience:** couples and clients judging style first, then availability and price;
  art directors looking for a fit.
- **Tone options:** Quiet and confident (default) · Warm and personal · Playful.
- **Schema type:** `ProfessionalService` with `additionalType` Wikidata photographer (sync.py
  adds it), or `LocalBusiness` for a studio.
- **Default pages:** single page; multi-page with several distinct portfolios.
- **Primary CTA options:** Check availability / enquire (default) · Book a call · Email.

## Required sections
1. Hero: name, what they shoot + where, a strong image slot.
2. Portfolio grid: owner images only; labelled placeholders until then.
3. Services/packages from facts (prices only if given).
4. About (from facts, first person if the owner writes that way).
5. Enquiry: email, phone, form via a form service.

## Optional sections (rank by fit)
How a shoot works (from facts) · Client list (only with permission) · FAQ · Studio
location (only if clients visit) · Prints/shop (real link).

## Category questions (ask at most 4, skip any answered)
- "What do you shoot most?" Weddings / Portraits and families / Commercial / Mixed.
- "Show prices?" "Starting at" prices (owner's) / On request (Recommended if none).
- "Do clients visit a studio?" Yes, show address and map / No, I travel (Recommended: no map).
- "Where are your best images?" Folder path to copy into public/images, or TODO slots.

## Imagery
Their work is the site. Keep chrome minimal, image slots large, captions small. Never stock
or AI images, never other photographers' work, never client photos without permission.

## Never invent
Prices · client names or brands · publications/features · awards · years shooting · packages
· reviews · gear lists.

## Design fit
Directions: `gallery` (default), `quiet-luxury`, `broadsheet`. Palettes: `darkroom`,
`gallery-white`, `estate-stone`, `rose-clay`.

## Copy notes
Very little copy. Captions: number, subject, place ("03 — Wedding, Porto").
