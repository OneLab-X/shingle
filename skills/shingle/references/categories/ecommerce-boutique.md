# Category: ecommerce-boutique

Independent shops with a physical store and/or online shop: clothing, gifts, books, plants,
homeware, jewellery, records.

- **Audience:** locals deciding to visit, online shoppers arriving from social media, gift
  buyers checking what the shop sells.
- **Tone options:** Curated and warm (default) · Playful · Refined.
- **Schema type:** most specific `Store` subtype (`ClothingStore`, `BookStore`, `GardenStore`,
  `JewelryStore`, `HomeGoodsStore`…); online-only `OnlineStore`.
- **Default pages:** single page. The checkout lives on the owner's shop platform (Shopify,
  Etsy, Big Cartel, Square): link to it. Shingle never builds a cart or takes payments.
- **Primary CTA options:** Shop online (only with a real shop URL) · Visit the shop (default for
  physical) · Follow on Instagram.

## Required sections
1. Hero: shop name, what they sell + where, visit/shop CTA.
2. What we stock: categories or featured items from facts (prices only if given).
3. Visit: hours, address, map (physical shops).
4. Contact and socials.

## Optional sections (rank by fit)
About/story · Brands/makers stocked (only stated) · Gift cards (real link) · Events/workshops ·
Delivery and returns (only stated policies) · Gallery.

## Category questions (ask at most 4, skip any answered)
- "Where do people buy?" In the shop (Recommended if address) / Online shop link / Both.
- "Feature specific products?" Yes, from a list the owner gives / Categories only (Recommended).
- "Delivery, click-and-collect, returns?" Enter stated policies / Leave out.
- "Which brands or makers to name?" Owner's list / None.

## Imagery
Products the owner photographed, the shopfront, the shelves. Square and 4:5 crops. Never
supplier or brand press images without permission, never stock products.

## Never invent
Products, prices, stock levels · brands carried · delivery times, free-shipping thresholds,
returns policies · discounts · awards · reviews · "sustainable"/"ethical" claims.

## Design fit
Directions: `handmade`, `gallery`, `quiet-luxury`, `poster`. Palettes: `boutique-plum`,
`espresso-cream`, `rose-clay`, `gallery-white`, `darkroom`.

## Copy notes
Specific nouns over adjectives. Make clear whether people can buy online or only in store.
