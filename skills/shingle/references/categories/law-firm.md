# Category: law-firm

Solicitors, attorneys, barristers' chambers, notaries, immigration advisers, legal clinics.

- **Audience:** individuals with a legal problem (often stressed, comparing firms), business
  clients, referrers. Many first visits come from searching the practice area + city.
- **Tone options:** Calm and plain-spoken (default) · Confident and direct · Formal and traditional.
- **Schema type:** `LegalService` (individual practitioners as `Attorney` only with names the
  owner provides). `areaServed`, `knowsAbout` from practice areas.
- **Default pages:** multi-page: Home, Practice areas (or one page per area when 4+), About,
  Contact. Single page for a sole practitioner with 1-2 areas.
- **Primary CTA options:** Book a consultation (default) · Call the office · Send an enquiry (form).

## Required sections
1. Hero: firm name, practice areas + location in plain words, how to get in touch.
2. Practice areas: only the owner's list, each with who it helps and typical matters.
3. How it works / first consultation: fee basis only if stated (free, fixed fee, hourly).
4. Contact: phone, email, office address and hours, enquiry form via a form service.
5. Legal notices required where the firm practises (regulator, registration number) as TODOs
   unless the owner provides them.

## Optional sections (rank by fit)
Team (names, roles, admissions from facts) · Languages spoken · Fees and funding (only stated) ·
FAQ · Insights/blog · Accessibility and remote meetings.

## Category questions (ask at most 4, skip any answered)
- "First consultation?" Free (only if true) / Fixed fee / Paid at hourly rate / Prefer not to say (Recommended).
- "Which regulator and registration number must appear?" Enter it / Leave a TODO (Recommended).
- "Who should the site speak to first?" Individuals (Recommended) / Businesses / Both.
- "Enquiries by form?" Yes via Formspree or Web3Forms / Phone and email only (Recommended).

## Imagery
Restraint. The office exterior, real team portraits, or none: type and rules do the work.
Never gavels, scales of justice, or handshake stock photos.

## Never invent
Case results, settlement amounts, win rates · client testimonials · years of experience ·
"leading", "top-rated", "expert" claims or directory badges · team members or admissions ·
fees · regulator names or numbers · "no win, no fee" unless stated.

## Compliance notes
Lawyer advertising rules are strict and vary (e.g. SRA in England and Wales, state bar rules
in the US): no misleading claims, often no testimonials or "specialist" wording, mandatory
regulatory statements. Keep copy factual and add a TODO asking the owner to confirm the
required disclaimers for their jurisdiction.

## Design fit
Directions: `broadsheet` (default), `swiss-grid`, `clinical-calm`. Palettes: `counsel-navy`,
`oxblood-ledger`, `harbor-blue`, `estate-stone`.

## Copy notes
Plain English. Describe problems the firm solves ("buying your first home", "a dispute with
your landlord") rather than Latin or statute names. No fear-based copy.
