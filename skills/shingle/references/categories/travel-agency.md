# Category: travel-agency

Travel agents, tour operators, trip planners, destination specialists, local tour guides.

- **Audience:** people dreaming and planning (browsing on a sofa), people ready to enquire,
  past clients.
- **Tone options:** Warm and expert (default) · Adventurous and playful · Refined.
- **Schema type:** `TravelAgency`. Tours may add `TouristTrip` items only from facts.
- **Default pages:** single page; multi-page with more than 6 trips or destinations.
- **Primary CTA options:** Plan my trip / enquire (default) · Call us · Book a consultation.

## Required sections
1. Hero: name, what kind of travel + where from/to, enquiry CTA.
2. Trips or destinations from `facts.trips` (names, length, highlights; prices only if given).
3. How planning works (from `facts.process`).
4. Contact + office hours; address only if the agency receives visitors.

## Optional sections (rank by fit)
About the planners (from facts) · Licences and bonding (ATOL, IATA, state registrations: only
the owner's numbers) · FAQ · Testimonials (owner-supplied) · Journal/blog.

## Category questions (ask at most 4, skip any answered)
- "What do you sell most?" Tailor-made trips (Recommended) / Group tours / Both.
- "Show prices?" "From" prices given by the owner / "Prices on request" (Recommended if none).
- "Licence or bonding numbers to show?" Enter / TODO (Recommended).
- "How do enquiries arrive?" Email and phone (Recommended) / Form via a form service / WhatsApp.

## Imagery
The owner's own trip photos (with rights) or illustration and maps. Wide, horizon-led crops.
Never stock beach photos or images of places the agency does not sell.

## Never invent
Prices · itineraries · hotel or airline partners · licence/bonding numbers · "best price"
guarantees · countries visited counts · reviews · visa or health advice.

## Design fit
Directions: `wayfinder` (default), `broadsheet`, `gallery`. Palettes: `lagoon`,
`terracotta-sun`, `meadow-hope`.

## Copy notes
Concrete: days, places, seasons. Make the next step clear: what happens after an enquiry.
