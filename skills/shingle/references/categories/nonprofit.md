# Category: nonprofit

Charities, community groups, food banks, shelters, clubs, associations, mutual aid, religious
community centres, campaigns.

- **Audience:** people who need help (first and most important), volunteers, donors,
  partners and press.
- **Tone options:** Warm and hopeful (default) · Urgent and direct · Calm and dignified.
- **Schema type:** `NGO` (or `Organization`); `nonprofitStatus` only if stated.
- **Default pages:** single page; multi-page for several programmes or a news section.
- **Primary CTA options:** Get help (default when the org serves people directly) · Volunteer ·
  Donate (only with a real donation link).

## Required sections
1. Hero: what the organisation does, for whom, where. The main CTA.
2. Get help: who can use the service, how, when, and where, in plain words.
3. Get involved: volunteer and donate routes that actually exist.
4. Contact, opening times, address/map if people visit.
5. Registration details (charity number, etc.) only from facts; else TODO.

## Optional sections (rank by fit)
Programmes/services · Impact (only owner-provided figures with a source/date) · Events ·
News · Partners and funders (only stated, with logo permission) · Accessibility and languages.

## Category questions (ask at most 4, skip any answered)
- "Who is the site mainly for?" People who need help (Recommended) / Volunteers / Donors.
- "Donation link?" Enter it (Stripe, PayPal, JustGiving, etc.) / No online donations yet.
- "Registration number to show?" Enter / TODO (Recommended).
- "Languages people need?" Local language(s) / Add a second language.

## Imagery
Real people from the community only with consent; otherwise illustration and colour. Avoid
"poverty imagery" and stock photos of suffering. Dignity first.

## Never invent
Impact numbers ("1,000 meals served") · registration numbers · tax-deductibility statements ·
partner or funder names · testimonials · founding year · staff or trustees.

## Design fit
Directions: `wayfinder`, `broadsheet`, `handmade`, `clinical-calm` (services). Palettes:
`meadow-hope`, `civic-red`, `olive-grove`, `sage-linen`, `lagoon`.

## Copy notes
Write for someone in a hard moment: short sentences, what to bring, what happens next. Put
"Get help" before "Donate".
